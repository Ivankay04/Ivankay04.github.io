Questions

Before
you book

Where is the freeride?

Near Ridder, on the ridges of the Western Altai in East Kazakhstan. Ridder is 120 km from Ust-Kamenogorsk.

How do I get to Ridder?

Fly to Ust-Kamenogorsk (Oskemen, UKK) via Almaty or Astana. Ridder is about two hours by road; we pick you up at the airport.

When is the season?

From mid-November to mid-April, snow permitting.

What level do I need?

You should ski or ride confidently. The guide picks slopes for the group — from first days off-piste to steeper terrain.

Can I rent equipment?

Avalanche gear and radios can be rented on site. Bring your own skis or snowboard.

Can you arrange accommodation and transfers?

Yes. We meet you at Ust-Kamenogorsk airport, book your stay in Ridder and drive you to the start every day.
