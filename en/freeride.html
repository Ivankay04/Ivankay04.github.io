Questions

Before
you book

Where is the freeride?

Near Ridder in the Western Altai, East Kazakhstan. The bases are Klimovka and Vosmyorka. Ridder is 120 km from Ust-Kamenogorsk.

How do I get there?

Fly to Ust-Kamenogorsk (Oskemen, UKK) via Almaty or Astana. We arrange the transfer from the airport to the base and back.

When is the season?

From mid-November to mid-April.

What is included?

A bed in a house at the base, breakfast, lunch box and dinner, a seat on the snowmobile or in the snowcat, a guide, avalanche gear (probe, shovel, transceiver) and banya.

How many people are needed?

Snowcat tours run with 10 or more, up to 12–14. Snowmobile tours run with 4 or more, up to 6. If there are fewer of you, write to us and we will find an option.
