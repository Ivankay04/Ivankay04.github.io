How the day goes

Half a day
in the mountains

The whole trip from Ridder takes 5–6 hours. We choose the starting point by the weather and where the vehicles are, and confirm it when we confirm your date.
01

Transfer

About 45 minutes from Ridder to the base in our 4WDs. The vehicles and guides are waiting there.

02

The climb

10–15 km across the mountains. About an hour by snowcat, faster by snowmobile.

03

Viewpoints

Stops on the way up for the views of the ridges and photos.

04

The pass

About 2,000 m. Tables, a grill, lunch and hot tea, time to walk around.

05

Descent

Back the same way to the base, then the transfer to Ridder.

Prices

Why the price depends
on group size

The cost of a trip — vehicles, drivers, guide — is almost the same whether 4 people go or 12. It is shared between the guests, so the bigger the group, the less each pays. The “from” price is for a full group. If there are fewer of you, we will quote an exact price for your group or offer a place in a shared weekend group.
What is included
  • Vehicles and fuel
  • Guide and snowmobile drivers
  • Transfer from Ridder to the start and back
  • Lunch at the top and hot tea
  • Gear at no extra cost
Not included: transfer from Ust-Kamenogorsk or from your hotel or resort — quoted on request.
